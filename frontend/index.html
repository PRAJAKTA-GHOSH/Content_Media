Content Ops / Generative AI

One brief becomes
three campaigns.
Every one learns.

Loop turns a single content brief into on-brand, natively bilingual assets for three channels — validates them against real platform limits, holds them for human approval, measures what shipped, and feeds the result back into the next brief.

Create

Genuinely different, per channel

Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn each get their own visual composition, tone, length and CTA. Bengali and English are both generated natively — never one translated from the other.

Control

Nothing publishes on its own

Every asset is checked against the destination platform's real constraints, then held until a human explicitly approves it. Reject and regenerate is a first-class path, not an afterthought.

Learn

Insight becomes the next brief

Published results are compared campaign-to-campaign, like for like. What worked is written back as a recommendation the next brief starts from — not left to sit on a dashboard.
